BloodNexus Brute Force & Appeal Update

Added:
1. Admin Security Center: high-risk brute-force alerts (same account/IP, rolling 10 minutes).
2. Alert shows account, failed-attempt count, IP, time window, View Activity and Block Account.
3. Permanently Blocked Accounts list with reason, blocked time and appeal status.
4. One-time appeal policy: one email can submit only one appeal.
5. After appeal submission, the email is redirected to Track Appeal.
6. Pending: waiting for admin.
7. Approved: account is unblocked.
8. Rejected: account remains blocked and the same email cannot submit another appeal.
9. Admin review logs are added to SecurityLog.

php artisan migrate
php artisan optimize:clear
